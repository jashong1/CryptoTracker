# Brainstorming

## New App Ideas - List

1. **Recipe Finder**: search recipes by ingredient and save favorites (TheMealDB API)
2. **Movie Watchlist**: browse trending movies and keep a watchlist (TMDB API)
3. **Weather Wardrobe**: daily forecast with outfit suggestions (OpenWeatherMap API)
4. **Pokédex**: browse and search Pokémon with a stats detail screen (PokéAPI)
5. **Book Tracker**: search books and track Reading / Want to Read / Done (Google Books API)
6. **CoinWatch (Crypto Tracker)**: live crypto prices, charts and pinned favorites (CoinGecko API)
7. **Local Events Finder**: concerts and events nearby, filtered by date (Ticketmaster API)
8. **Random Activity Generator**: activity ideas when bored, filtered by type (Bored API)

## Top 3 New App Ideas

1. CoinWatch (Crypto Tracker)
2. Recipe Finder
3. Movie Watchlist

## New App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

1. **CoinWatch**
   - **Description:** Track live cryptocurrency prices, view charts, and pin favorite coins.
   - **Category:** Finance
   - **Mobile:** Very mobile, since people check prices on the go many times a day.
   - **Story:** Clear value: a clean, simple way to follow the coins you care about.
   - **Market:** Large. Crypto has millions of casual and hobby investors.
   - **Habit:** High. Prices change constantly, so users open it many times a day.
   - **Scope:** Moderate. The core list, search and details are very doable, and the free CoinGecko API needs no key.

2. **Recipe Finder**
   - **Description:** Search meals by ingredient or category and save favorites.
   - **Category:** Food & Lifestyle
   - **Mobile:** Useful in the kitchen or at the grocery store.
   - **Story:** Helps people decide what to cook with what they have.
   - **Market:** Very large. Everyone cooks.
   - **Habit:** Medium. Used a few times a week around meals.
   - **Scope:** Easy. TheMealDB is free with no key.

3. **Movie Watchlist**
   - **Description:** Browse trending and upcoming movies and save a watchlist.
   - **Category:** Entertainment
   - **Mobile:** Handy when deciding what to watch with friends.
   - **Story:** Keeps track of movies you want to see.
   - **Market:** Large. Movie fans of all ages.
   - **Habit:** Medium. Used on weekends or when picking a movie.
   - **Scope:** Moderate. TMDB requires a free API key.

## Final Pick

**CoinWatch.** It scored highest on habit and mobile use, it uses a free API that needs no key, and it naturally fits every requirement: an API, a RecyclerView list, custom styling (green/red price colors) and multiple interactions (tap, long-press, search text entry, pull to refresh).
